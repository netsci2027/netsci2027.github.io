Kristina Lerman is a Professor of Informatics at Indiana University’s Luddy School of Informatics, Computing and Engineering. Trained as a physicist, she uses AI and network science to explore how algorithms and online platforms shape social behavior, human psychology, and access to information and social support. Her work has been covered by the Washington Post, Wall Street Journal, The Atlantic, and AfterBabel. She is a fellow of AAAI.
