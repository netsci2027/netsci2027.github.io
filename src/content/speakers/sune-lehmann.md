Sune Lehmann is Professor of Networks and Complexity Science at DTU Compute, Technical University of Denmark, and Professor of Social Data Science at the University of Copenhagen. He studies the structure and dynamics of social networks, from link communities and temporal networks to complex contagion, human mobility and nation-scale networks built from Danish registry data. He was General Chair of NetSci 2013.
