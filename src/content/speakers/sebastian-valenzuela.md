Sebastián Valenzuela (PhD, University of Texas at Austin) is Professor in the School of Communications at Pontifical Catholic University of Chile, Associate Researcher with the Millennium Institute for Foundational Research on Data (IMFD) in Chile, and Chief Science Officer of the Swiss-based International Panel on the Information Environment (IPIE). He is associate editor for Human Communication Research and sits on the editorial board of several top-tier communication journals. He is an expert in political communication, misinformation, and algorithmic media, topics on which he has authored more than 100 peer-reviewed publications, including the third edition of Setting the Agenda: The News Media and Public Opinion (Polity Press, 2021). His research has received awards from the International Communication Association (ICA), the Association for Education in Journalism and Mass Communication (AEJMC), and the World Association for Public Opinion Research (WAPOR). He was elected ICA Fellow in 2025. He has held visiting research positions at the universities of Wisconsin-Madison (School of Journalism &amp; Mass Communication) and Amsterdam (ASCoR).
