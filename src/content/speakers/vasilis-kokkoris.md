Vasilis Kokkoris is an Associate Professor at the Amsterdam Institute for Life and Environment (A-LIFE) at Vrije Universiteit (VU) Amsterdam. He studies the hidden world of fungi living in soil—especially the vast underground networks that connect plants and help ecosystems thrive. Using advanced microscopy and imaging techniques, his work explores how these fungi operate across scales, from individual cells to entire ecosystems, and how they contribute to sustainable agriculture and climate resilience. Vasilis is also known for revealing the beauty of this invisible world through award-winning microscopy. His images have received international recognition, including prizes such as the Nikon Small World (2020) and Olympus Image of the Year (2021), and have helped bring the science of fungi to a broader audience. Alongside his research, he has received several prestigious scientific honors, including early-career awards for his contributions to science and pedagogy, and major research funding to further explore how these remarkable organisms shape life on Earth.
